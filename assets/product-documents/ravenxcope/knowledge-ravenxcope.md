# RavenXcope Product Knowledge

## Product and audience

RavenXcope is a distributed Network Intrusion Detection System (NIDS) platform focused on asset visibility and network threat analysis. It connects alerts with host, network and asset-criticality context to help teams understand affected assets and prioritize review. Asset inventory, alert analytics, IP flow visualization and a risk matrix support monitoring and investigation.

Target readers: network managers, security analysts, SOC teams and IT infrastructure managers. Key message: connect network alerts to asset context and criticality to support prioritization. See `CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md` for the owner-provided Indonesian brochure copy.

## Capabilities

1. **Host and network inventory:** centralized inventory of hosts and networks, including hostnames, IP addresses or CIDR ranges, associated networks, tags, status and criticality.
2. **Asset-context threat analysis:** review alerts with the criticality of associated source and destination assets to distinguish findings needing earlier review.
3. **Risk prioritization:** a risk matrix and overall criticality indicators combine alert severity with asset criticality, helping teams focus on alerts associated with important assets.
4. **Activity and threat-pattern analytics:** inspect alert trends, priority distribution, threat classifications, protocols and frequently observed source and destination IP addresses.
5. **IP flow visualization:** visualize source-to-destination communications to investigate connection patterns associated with security alerts.
6. **Detailed investigation and sensor context:** filter by time, sensor, priority, classification, alert message, IP address and port; review alerts by the monitoring point in the distributed NIDS environment.
7. **False-positive review and marking:** inspect alert details and use available controls to mark false positives, distinguishing active alerts from those marked for exclusion from review focus.

## Intended benefits

Recognize hosts and networks in the inventory; connect alerts to associated assets; prioritize review by severity and asset criticality; investigate network communications and manage triage outcomes.
