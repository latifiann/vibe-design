## A. Brand and visual consistency

- [ ] Orange-red/vermilion brand color follows the supplied Ravenxcope logo.
- [ ] No pure-red/cyan/mint palette substitution.
- [ ] Logo is not distorted, recolored, redrawn, or decorated.
- [ ] Typography uses the approved font system.
- [ ] Front and back share the same visual language.
- [ ] Geometric elements are consistent and restrained.
- [ ] No generic AI cybersecurity clichés.
- [ ] No unlicensed or unattributed assets.

## B. Product truthfulness

- [ ] All screenshots are genuine Ravenxcope screenshots.
- [ ] All architecture labels and arrows are validated.
- [ ] All features described are available and approved for publication.
- [ ] No invented statistics, customer logos, certifications, or performance claims.
- [ ] The NIDS category and claims match current RavenXcope knowledge; no IPS or automatic-blocking claims.
- [ ] Screenshots taken at different times are not presented as one snapshot; UI data and colors are not falsified.
- [ ] Publication permission for screenshots and visible information is confirmed; new assets are checked separately. The current screenshots are owner-approved for publication.
- [ ] QR code destination is final and verified.
- [ ] Contact information is official.

## C. Layout and readability

- [ ] Both sides use A5 portrait trim dimensions.
- [ ] Bleed is present according to printer requirements.
- [ ] Important content stays inside safe margins.
- [ ] Headline is readable at actual A5 size.
- [ ] Body text is preferably at least 9.5 pt.
- [ ] Captions are not used for essential information.
- [ ] Contrast is sufficient.
- [ ] No text is clipped or hidden behind graphics.
- [ ] QR code has sufficient quiet zone and scans from a physical proof.
- [ ] No unnecessary decorative elements fill negative space.

## D. Technical prepress

- [ ] Resolution is 300 DPI for raster content at final size.
- [ ] CMYK conversion uses the printer's supplied ICC profile.
- [ ] Black values follow the printer's recommendation.
- [ ] Fonts are embedded or outlined in the final PDF workflow as appropriate.
- [ ] Bleed and trim boxes are correct in the final PDF if PDF is required.
- [ ] Front/back orientation is correct.
- [ ] Final PDF is inspected after export, not only before export.
- [ ] A physical proof is reviewed before bulk printing.

## E. Approval gates

Gate 1 — Content approval:
- Product description, claims, features, contacts, CTA, and language approved.

Gate 2 — Design approval:
- Front/back composition, typography, color, screenshots, and diagrams approved.

Gate 3 — Print approval:
- Printer specifications, color profile, bleed, final PDF/export, and physical proof approved.

Do not skip a gate.

## F. Review report

Report critical, major and minor issues; facts requiring confirmation; missing assets; and print blockers. Recommend specific corrections without redesigning the entire brochure unless requested. Publication permission alone is not content, design or print approval. Historical lessons: `research/LEARNING.md`.
