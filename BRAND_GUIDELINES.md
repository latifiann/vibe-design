# RavenXcope Brand Guidelines

## Character and logo

The brand should feel confident, precise, technical, credible, enterprise-ready and modern without excessive futurism. Avoid gaming, crypto, sci-fi and generic cybersecurity aesthetics. Preserve the logo tagline exactly: **EVERY PACKET MATTERS**.

Use the owner-approved transparent PNGs unchanged: `assets/ravenxcope-logo-transparan.png` (full lockup) and `assets/ravenxcope-ikon-transparan.png` (symbol). Both are raster, not vector masters; check effective resolution at print size and request an official SVG/PDF if available. Their color appearances differ; do not reconcile them by recoloring. Use the full lockup when name recognition matters; use the symbol alone only in constrained space with clear brand context. Maintain clear space of at least the height of the logo's eye/internal negative space, or follow official rules if supplied.

Never redraw, trace, distort, tilt, compress or stretch the logo; change its spacing, typography or color; automatically remove backgrounds; or add glow, bevel, extrusion, 3D, decorative shadow, outline or gradient. Screenshot cyan is part of the original product UI, not the brochure accent; preserve it unchanged.

`#DB241C` is a sampled orange-red/vermilion, not pure red; an official brand master takes precedence if provided. Start with 75–85% dark neutrals, 10–15% off-white/grey and 5–10% orange-red. Use accents selectively, not cyan/mint as a default. Avoid conspicuous red-orange gradients; subtle gradients are limited to non-logo decoration. Maintain sufficient text contrast.

## Typography and shapes

Use at most two main font families: Space Grotesk, Manrope or an available grotesque sans for headlines; Inter or a neutral sans for body. IBM Plex Mono is optional for technical labels, numbers and metadata; do not add a third family merely to look technical. Start with headline 26–36 pt, section heading 15–20 pt, body 9.5–11 pt and nonessential captions 8–9 pt. Avoid thin or cramped body text. Use sentence case except short labels, eyebrows and the official tagline.

Allowed shapes: modular rectangular grids, cropped geometric planes, thin technical lines, subtle dot matrices, consistent abstract shapes informed by the logo, section numbers and small labels. Do not recreate the logo. Avoid random neon circuits, glowing shields or eyes, repeated generic hexagons, fake terminal wallpaper, unrelated holographic interfaces and meaningless network nodes. Use consistent sizing, placement and repetition.
