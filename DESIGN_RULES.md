# Design Rules — Dark Enterprise Cybersecurity Editorial

## v07 owner direction

Option B may use authentic screenshots without a laptop. This supersedes historical screenshot-free B notes below. See `research/LEARNING.md` for actual candidate status.

## Direction and composition

Draw on Fortra DSPM's editorial hierarchy, dark treatment and product-led storytelling, and ITSEC Asia's corporate messaging and confident typography. Never copy their assets or layouts. The result should feel professional rather than flashy, premium rather than extravagant, technical without excess jargon, distinctive rather than templated, and product-led.

Prioritize a clear message, actual product screenshot, correct architecture if relevant, typography, contextual photography and then brand geometry. Decoration must not dominate the message. Use an asymmetric editorial grid, consistent alignment and generous negative space. Use one hero visual on the front; vary the back with concise feature blocks and CTA. Avoid filling every gap with patterns, centered symmetry, unnecessary technical labels and repeated UI-like cards.

## Product screenshots

Use genuine RavenXcope screenshots only. The owner approved the eight supplied screenshots and visible data for publication; assess new assets separately. Do not add fabricated charts, numbers, badges or alerts, or alter data meaning. Crop only when context remains accurate. Preserve original UI colors and labels. Captures from different times are not one snapshot and their numbers are not performance claims. Prefer analytics capture `192316` and risk matrix capture `192442`; see `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`. If no appropriate screenshot is available, use a clearly marked `ACTUAL PRODUCT SCREENSHOT REQUIRED` placeholder, never generated UI.

## Architecture and photography

Diagrams must come from actual architecture or be approved by the product owner. Verify labels, arrow directions, components and relationships. Use simple consistent vectors; do not create meaningless decorative network nodes. If topology is unavailable, do not invent or publish an architecture diagram; conceptual diagrams require owner validation before publication.

Use photography only when relevant: security operations, data centers, enterprise networks, or relevant critical infrastructure. Prefer owned images or clearly licensed stock. Avoid hooded hackers, masks, skulls, green-code keyboards, neon cities, AI brains, glowing 3D shields and irrelevant imagery.

## Image policy and consistency

No new generated UI, logos, icons, diagrams or rasterized posters with text. Owner explicitly authorized the two supplied v04 hero illustrations in `assets/generated/` as separate decorative layers; this is a limited exception, not permission for further generation. A combines its background with authentic screenshots; B deliberately uses no screenshots. Illustrations do not represent data, architecture, devices or factual claims. Feature illustrations are manually constructed editable vector shapes. Non-generative crop, exposure, contrast, masks and resizing of approved decorative images are permitted. Preserve sources and provenance; never alter screenshot data or UI colors.

Owner additionally authorized the supplied `assets/generated/ravenxcope-laptop-greenscreen.png` as a generic presentation mockup for v05 option A. Its screen is replaced non-generatively with genuine screenshot content on separate layers. This is not a RavenXcope device or an authorization for further generated assets. Option B remains screenshot-free.

Keep one grid, spacing scale, type system, icon style and geometric language; use the orange-red accent and headers, footers and labels consistently across both sides.
